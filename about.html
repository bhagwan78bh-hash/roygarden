Our Story

Built by gardeners, for gardeners. Roygarden started in a small backyard workshop and grew into a community of makers.

🌿

From a backyard to a brand

In 2019, our founder Roy started modifying old hand tools in his garage to make them easier on the wrists. Friends asked for copies. Then neighbors. Then strangers online. Today Roygarden ships to 40+ countries.

We believe gardening should be joyful, not exhausting. Every tool we design is tested in real gardens by real people before it earns the Roygarden name.

Our Values

  • Quality first. We choose materials that outlast seasons, not trends.
  • Design for hands. Ergonomics matter. Tools should feel like an extension of you.
  • Sustainability. Recyclable packaging, responsibly sourced wood, and repairable parts.
  • Community. We share guides, host workshops, and listen to every review.
